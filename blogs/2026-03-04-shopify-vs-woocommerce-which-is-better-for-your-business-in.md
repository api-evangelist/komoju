Shopify and WooCommerce together power millions of active online stores worldwide. As of 2025, WooCommerce leads in overall store count, with over 4.4 million live sites—largely due to its flexibility and integration with WordPress. Shopify, meanwhile, runs nearly 2.85 million stores thanks to its easy setup, mobile-ready themes, and global infrastructure.
