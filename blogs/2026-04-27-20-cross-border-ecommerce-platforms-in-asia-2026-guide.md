Cross-border eCommerce is the process of selling products or services online to customers in other countries. Cross-border eCommerce empowers brands to scale globally by reaching customers in high-growth international markets. Unlike domestic eCommerce, these transactions often involve international logistics, currency conversion, and cross-border payment processing.
