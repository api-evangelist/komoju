Overselling happens when a business accepts more orders than its available inventory. In the eCommerce world, this mismatch between digital data and physical stock can lead to significant operational costs and harm both customer satisfaction and brand loyalty. In this article, we will explain the causes of overselling and provide concrete strategies to prevent it.
